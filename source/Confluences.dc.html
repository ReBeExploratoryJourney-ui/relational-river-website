Confluences

Many ways in

The Relational River is one way in: a meshwork of remembrance and relational development, where we slow down and grow real relationships with the Beings, people and places around us.

Other currents tend other parts of how we find each other and work together: mapping, gathering knowledge, organising locally. Where streams meet, the water grows. We point to these kindred currents with gratitude, and say plainly how the River relates to each.

How the River meets other currents

  • We link to kindred work rather than copy it. Each current here is best placed to tend its own part.
  • We share the River’s offerings in open formats, so other maps and directories can find them without anyone joining another platform.
  • Each current below says whether any partnership is involved. Unless it says so, there is none.

Gathering and organising

Hylo

An open-source home for communities to build trust, share knowledge, make sense together and coordinate bioregional action. River circles already meet here, in the Institute of Relational Being’s group, alongside kindred communities such as the Planetary Health Alliance’s member network.

Knowledge for connectors

Catalist

A knowledge ecosystem for network weavers, event organisers and ecosystem mappers: a personal library, community spaces and a global map, so insight can flow between people and networks. Catalist seeded OMNI-Mapping, below.

Relationship: none

An open protocol

Murmurations

Like starlings moving together without a leader: an open protocol from the Open Co-op that lets groups share data across platforms and networks, while each keeps its own.

Relationship: the River shares its offerings this way

Maps that build on each other

OMNI-Mapping

A project of the Buckminster Fuller Institute’s Design Lab, inspired by Fuller’s design science, helping organisations make maps that are useful where they are and reusable everywhere.

Relationship: none

Local, face to face

Karrot

A free, open-source tool for grassroots groups who coordinate face-to-face activities on a local, voluntary basis. It grew from food-saving circles and now serves many kinds of community.

Relationship: none

Shared openly

The River’s offerings, readable by other maps

The River, the Canal and each open Spring are published as small Murmurations profiles, hosted by the River itself. Any map or directory that reads Murmurations can find them through the Murmurations index. A Spring’s profile always names and links its makers, and lets go on its own once the Spring closes.

If your offering appears in our Springs and you publish your own profile, tell us and we will point to yours instead.
