The source

Arrive wherever you are.

The Relational River is a free guide to the living world and the many people already tending it. There is nothing to know first and nothing to get right. A few gentle questions help us suggest where to begin.

All our ancestors once lived in close relationship with the land and ecology they were a part of, and that belonging is still here for each of us.

No account needed. Nothing is scored. You can always choose differently.

Your Boat

Set off with your own Boat

Your Boat is your own private vessel on the River. It carries your answers from the Source, the practices you tend, a cycle with Moon, and a constellation of the relationships that make up your “we”. No account is needed, and you choose how it is kept.
