The capital journey · open to everyone

Money is part of the living world

Every coin and account is a claim on soil, water, energy and other people's work. This journey is for anyone who wants a new relationship with money, and especially for those who steward wealth: philanthropists, family offices, foundations. It teaches and introduces. It never sells.

Money is "just a marker, a claim on the real physical work."

Nate Hagens, The Great Simplification

Care around money

The River informs and introduces; it never sells, ranks projects for profit or takes a cut that bends what you see. Gifts flow through trusted hosts such as Reculture. Any investment happens elsewhere, through licensed platforms or directly between parties with their own advisers. Communities keep a real voice in how money reaching their place is used.

With gratitude to Nate Hagens, Indy Johar, Dark Matter Labs and the BioFi Project, whose work this journey points to.
