The Canal

Where people guide the flow

A river finds its own way. A canal is water guided by people who are helping to reveal potentially beneficial and regenerative ways forward, dug by hand and with care. No one here claims to know the way: each canal is offered with curiosity and humility by a named keeper, who says why they dug it.

Canals have locks. A lock lifts a boat to a higher level, one chamber at a time, only when you are ready. At every lock there is a quiet way back to the wild River.

How canals are kept

  • Every canal is signed by its keeper, with their reason for guiding the water this way.
  • Every canal says plainly whether any partnership, referral or money is involved.
  • Canals point toward offerings. The River introduces; it never sells.
Canal one · kept by Joe Culhane

From relational practice to relational business

For founders, leaders and anyone approaching the edges of their own discovery: people already living relationally who feel ready to let it change how they lead, and how they navigate the world of business.
