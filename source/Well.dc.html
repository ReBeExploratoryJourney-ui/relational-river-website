The Well

The Well of Wonder, Wit, Wisdom and Wealth

Beneath every river lies groundwater, and a well is where a community draws on it together. Here, people pour in what they can offer a more vibrant, regenerative and relational future: their skills, experience, passions and ideas. Others draw from the well to resource the projects they care about, and the River helps gather the people who could make them happen.

Everyone who pours in can also say what they need, so support can flow both ways, in money and in many other ways.

An old folk tale tells of travellers who arrive in a hungry village with only a cooking pot and a stone. They set the pot to boil and say they are making stone soup. One neighbour adds a carrot, another a handful of herbs, another an onion, until there is a soup that feeds everyone. The well works the same way: no one has to bring everything, only what they have.

What flows into the well

Wonder

Visions, ideas and questions

The future you can picture, the idea that won’t leave you alone, the question worth gathering people around.

Wit

Skills and craft

What your hands and mind know how to do: building, growing, cooking, facilitating, designing, coding, mending, teaching.

Wisdom

Experience and lived knowledge

Years with a place or a practice, lessons learned the hard way, and the guidance of elders.

Wealth

Resources to share

Money, and also land, space, tools, transport, time and networks. Wealth is anything that can help something living grow.

Reciprocity, in many currencies

Money is one current among many. Each offering says which kinds of exchange it welcomes, and each person can name what they need, so support can circle back in the form that truly helps.

Gift

Freely given

Offered for the love of the place or the work, with nothing asked in return.

Time exchange

An hour for an hour

Every hour given is honoured equally, whoever gives it, and can be drawn on later.

Skill swap

Your craft for mine

A website for a vegetable garden, a workshop for a weekend of help on the land.

Paid

Fair pay for good work

Many people need income to keep giving their best. Paid offerings are welcome and honoured.

For funders, foundations and family offices

Wealth that listens

The well can show where the capacity to act already exists, and where a little money would unlock it: a constellation of skilled, willing people in a bioregion, ready to begin and waiting only for resources.

Instead of funding a single organisation from afar, you can resource a whole constellation, meet its people as relationships, and see the skills, care and local knowledge your money joins.

  • See constellations, not profiles. Funders see what a constellation can do and needs, never people’s private details, until those people choose to meet.
  • Fund the gaps. The well shows which skills and resources are still missing in a place.
  • Begin with learning. The capital journey is a good first step before giving.
  • Open the capital journey

What the well can reveal

Imagine hundreds, then thousands, of people pouring in their skills and visions. Seen together, the well becomes a living picture of what is possible, in the spirit of ecological design and systems thinking: patterns, relationships and potential that no one could see alone.

How the well keeps care

Your offering is yours

  • Private by default. You choose who can see your offering, and you can change or withdraw it at any time.
  • The River suggests; people decide. No one joins a constellation without saying yes to an invitation.
  • No ranking or scoring of people, ever. Every kind of offering is honoured.
  • Only what an offering needs is asked for. There is no tracking and nothing is sold.
  • Stewards tend the well, help new people pour in, and keep it a kind place.

Earth is every human’s first home, and it has always been tended by many hands. The well is one more way for those hands to find each other.
