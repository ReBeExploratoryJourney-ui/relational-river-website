Care agreements

How we moor together

  • Be kind and honest about who you are and what you are working on.
  • Share only your own words and work, and no one else’s personal details.
  • The Harbour is for connecting and collaborating, not for advertising, selling or recruiting into unrelated schemes.
  • Meet people on Hylo with the same care, and respect a quiet “no, thank you”.
  • Honour Indigenous knowledge and its keepers. Stories, ceremonies and teachings are shared only with their consent.
  • Stewards from the Institute of Relational Being look over the Harbour regularly, and may rest a card out of view if it does not follow these agreements.
  • If three people raise a concern about a card within a week, it rests out of view until a steward has looked at it.
  • You can update your card or leave the Harbour at any time, and leaving takes effect straight away.

The Harbour is part of the River’s privacy promise. Questions or concerns can also go to the Institute of Relational Being’s group on Hylo.
