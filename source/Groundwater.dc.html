How the River weaves threads

The River notices; stewards tend; organisations decide

From open sources. The River reads what organisations choose to publish openly: open maps and directories, public groups and events, and their own websites. It weaves a thread when two or more share a place, a watershed or a kind of work.

Two kinds of thread. A documented connection is one the organisations themselves describe. Noticed by the River is a resonance: it does not claim they already know each other, only that they might be glad to.

Stewards feed in. New threads appear as noticed by the River. River stewards can offer threads they know of, with sources, and a regular steward tending them all is the hope as the River grows.

People stay private. Only organisations and projects are threaded here. Connections between people stay quiet and consent-based: no one is ever connected without choosing it.

Corrections are welcome. Any organisation can ask for a thread to be corrected or removed, and it will be. Last gathered: {{threadsAsOf}}.
