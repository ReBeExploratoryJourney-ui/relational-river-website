An idea from the Institute of Relational Being

Relational Resource Departments

Alongside human resources, a small group that tends an organisation’s relationships with people, place and the more-than-human world: the creek behind the office, the Beings in its supply chain, the bioregion it belongs to.

It can begin very simply, with a few colleagues and a monthly walk.

  1. Find two or three colleagues who feel the same pull.
  2. Begin with a monthly walk, or a meeting that starts outside.
  3. Bring one small practice into ordinary work, such as a minute of noticing before decisions.
  4. Share what you learn with others, so the idea can grow.

Kinship in how we lead

When the living world has a voice in decisions

Kincentric Leadership offers an open toolkit for leading in kinship with all life: eight principles, thirty-four capacities, twelve case studies and more than a hundred practices for organisations, communities and movements.

With The Repatterning Collective, they host the Co-creation with the More-than-Human sandbox: an hour of dialogue roughly once a month on approaches such as interspecies councils, nature on the board and rights of nature, each followed by a written synthesis anyone can read.
