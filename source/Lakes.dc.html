What a circle can feel like

1 · Arrive

We open with the circle’s agreements and a round of introductions: who we are, and a Being we are in relationship with.

2 · Listen

One person speaks at a time, from the heart. Others listen without fixing, advising or commenting.

3 · Practise

Between gatherings, each of us carries a small practice outside: a Sit Spot, a picture made together, an evening with Moon.

4 · Return

We close by naming what we are carrying onward, and thank one another and the land.

Held with care

Good circles have a host and shared agreements. What is shared in a circle stays in the circle, and stories are only passed on with permission. You can always step back, and nothing is ever required of you. If a circle doesn’t feel like this, it is fine to look for another.
