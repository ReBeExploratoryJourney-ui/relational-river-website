Your Boat

Your own vessel on the River. It carries what you gather as you go: where you began, the practices you are tending, and the Beings and people in your “we”. Come back whenever you like. It fills in slowly, the way relationships do.

Build your Boat

Choose how your Boat is kept. There is no wrong choice, and you can change your mind later.

On this device

Keep it on this phone or computer

  • Nothing leaves your device, and no account is needed.
  • It stays when you close or refresh the page.
  • It is lost if you clear your browser’s data, browse privately, or switch devices. Safari on iPhone may also clear it after about a week without a visit.
  • You can download a copy at any time, as a safety line.
With your email

Keep it with an IRB account

  • Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email. There is no password to remember, and no one can open your Boat without access to your inbox.
  • Your Boat sails with you, from phone to computer and back.
  • The same account can also open ReBe, the Institute’s app, if you would like it to. You never need one to use the other.
  • Already have a Boat with your email? Sign in here and it comes aboard this device.
