Learning your bioregion

Where does your watershed begin and end?

A bioregion is a place shaped by its waters, soils, climate and living communities, rather than by lines on a political map. These companions help you see yours, and meet others learning theirs.

Maps

Bioregional Maps

Maps of North America’s ecological regions, biomes and watersheds, made for learning, research and the wall at home. A lovely way to find the shape of the place you live.

Explore Bioregional Maps
A worldwide pathway

Bioregional Earth

A pathway for regenerating Earth one bioregion at a time, growing from Joe Brewer’s The Design Pathway for Regenerating Earth and the many people weaving with him. Its Dandelion strategy hopes for a thousand Bioregional Learning Centres by 2035.

Visit Bioregional Earth
Imagination and design

Ecological Design Collective

A community for radical ecological imagination and collaboration, with roots in Baltimore and relations around the world: talks, workshops, exhibitions, and Litter, its journal of essays and conversations.
