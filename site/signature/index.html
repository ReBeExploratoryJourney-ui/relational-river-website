Your Relational River email signature

Change the words if you like, press Copy my signature, then paste it into your email signature settings.

Where to paste it

  1. Gmail (on a computer): Settings, See all settings, General, Signature. Paste into the box, then Save Changes at the bottom.
  2. Apple Mail: Settings, Signatures. Untick “Always match my default message font”, then paste.
  3. Outlook: Settings, Signatures. Paste, then save.

If the copy button doesn’t work in your browser, select everything inside the white box and copy it yourself.

The logo lives on relationalriver.org, so it shows in each email without being attached. Clicking it opens the River.
