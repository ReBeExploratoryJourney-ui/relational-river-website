# What belongs in the Springs

Springs are time-sensitive offerings (cohorts, courses, gatherings, retreats, calls for participation) that rise up in the Relational River. They can be free, by donation or paid. Whether gathered by hand or noticed automatically, each spring should be in resonance with the ethos of the Institute of Relational Being.

## A spring belongs when it

- Deepens relationship with the living world, treating rivers, trees, creatures, places and Moon as Beings to be in relationship with, not as resources or backdrops.
- Supports reciprocity and care between humans and the rest of nature, and among people.
- Is grounded in place, community or living-systems understanding (for example regenerative practice, ecological grief and gratitude, bioregional work, land-based learning, relational ways of seeing).
- Honours Indigenous peoples. Indigenous-led offerings are shared in their own words, linking to their own sites. Offerings that teach ceremony or traditional knowledge appear only when they are clearly led or endorsed by the keepers of that knowledge.
- Is honest and transparent: a real organisation or person, a clear description, a clear price or cost note, and a working link.
- Makes room for people with fewer resources where it can (free places, sliding scales, scholarships), though paid offerings are welcome.

## A spring does not belong when it

- Sells extraction, greenwashing, carbon offsets as a substitute for change, or "nature" as a luxury product.
- Uses high-pressure sales, guaranteed outcomes, or fear to sell.
- Appropriates Indigenous ceremony, language or knowledge without the consent of its keepers.
- Is primarily marketing, lead generation, multi-level marketing, or speculative investment.
- Has no closing date, an unclear organiser, or no way to check it.

## How springs are kept

- Every spring has a closing date; the River hides it automatically once that date passes.
- Each spring says how it reached the River ("Shared by …" or "Noticed by the River").
- Springs found automatically come only from public pages, are described in the River's own words with a link to the source, and are reviewed against this page.
- Anyone can suggest a spring in the Institute of Relational Being's group on Hylo, and any organisation can ask for its spring to be changed or removed.
