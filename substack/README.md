# Springs from the Relational River: the weekly Substack note

Each Tuesday the scheduled "gather new Springs" run also drafts this week's note as a Claude Doc for IRB to review, edit and paste into Substack. A markdown copy is saved here as `substack/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, and `substack/log.md` records which organisation was illuminated each week so the spotlight keeps rotating.

## Shape of each issue (about 400 to 600 words)

1. **Good present to you** – two or three warm sentences: what this week's springs offer, and one line inviting readers into the Relational River (https://relationalriver.org). Weave in "All our ancestors once lived in close relationship with the land and ecology they were a part of" only where it fits naturally. Do not use the phrase "Earth is every human's first home".
2. **Springs bubbling up** – the open springs closing soonest (up to 6), as a table: spring (linked) · when and where · cost · enrol by. A line linking to https://relationalriver.org/springs.
3. **Illuminating** – one like-hearted organisation celebrated, chosen from tools/partners.py (or a host of this week's springs), not repeated within the last 8 weeks. Their own words where possible, linked to their site, credited by name.
4. **A thread the River noticed** – one connection from tools/threads.py, in a few sentences, linking to https://relationalriver.org/groundwater.
5. **A small practice for the week** – one practice from the Streams, Eddies or Moon pages, matched to the season or the Moon where possible.
6. **Flow with us** – wander the River; share a spring in IRB's Hylo group (https://www.hylo.com/groups/institute-of-relational-being); share this note.

## Voice

Warm, unhurried, plain English, no hype. Beings are they/them. "Moon", not "the moon". Indigenous voices are honoured and illuminated wherever possible, in their own words and linked to their own sites. Every organisation is credited and linked.
