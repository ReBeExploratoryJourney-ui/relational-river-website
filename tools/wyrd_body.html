Wyrd Bay

Wyrd is an old word. In Old English it named the turning of what comes to be: a weave of becoming that no one holds alone. This bay is for those who work with and within that becoming. Liminal agents, Wyrd Workers and anyone who quests are welcome here.

Come to cultivate connections, take up a quest with others, or sit alone by the water. Both are welcome, and you can change your mind as the tide does.

Nothing here knows the way. That is rather the point.

A word about a crowded word

“Liminal” has been carried into many rooms lately. In the Bay we try to use it with care, remembering where it came from: the limen, the threshold, in the rites of passage that Arnold van Gennep described, and the betwixt-and-between time that Victor Turner explored. A threshold is not a brand. It is where you are not yet who you will be.

The lantern test

Enchantment, or glamour?

Patrick Curry suggests that the counterfeit of enchantment is glamour: wonder that has been manufactured to sell something or to steer us. Before anything washes up in the Bay, we hold a lantern to it and ask:

  • Does it come from real depth of practice or lineage, rather than borrowing the word “liminal”?
  • Does it keep the mystery open, rather than selling certainty or a shortcut?
  • Is it entangled, weaving humans into the rest of nature and the cosmos?
  • Does it honour the keepers of the knowledge it draws on, and leave ceremony with those it belongs to?
  • Does it leave you more curious and more kind?

Know of something that would pass? Bring it to the Institute of Relational Being’s group on Hylo, and say it came from the Bay.
